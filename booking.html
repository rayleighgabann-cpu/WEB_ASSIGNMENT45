Booking FAQ

Is there a deposit for VIP cabins or halls?

Standard tables require no deposit. VIP cabins and banquet halls require a refundable deposit starting from 30,000 ₸, which goes entirely toward your order.

Can we pre-order banquet dishes?

Yes! For groups of 8 or more, we recommend pre-ordering whole besbarmak platters and samovar tea sets at least 24 hours in advance.

Vegetarian & child-friendly options?

We offer vegetable plov, fresh salads, highchairs, a kids menu, and accept Cash, Visa, Mastercard, and Kaspi QR.
